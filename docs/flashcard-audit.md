# Flashcard audit (October 2026)

Every deck was rebuilt against its reading using a two-pass method:

1. **Section audit.** List the major concepts in each reading section, rank them
   Must know (MK) / Should know (SK) / Reference only (Ref), and name what should
   *not* become a card.
2. **Card generation.** Cards come only from MK and selected SK concepts, then the
   deck is checked as a whole for coverage, balance, staging framework over
   subdivisions, number discipline, redundancy, and Rad Onc relevance.

Card rules applied everywhere: one question = one gradable answer; no long
"list everything" answers; exact numbers only when the number defines a
treatment, cutoff, or constraint; Extra holds context that does not need to be
memorized. Card ids were kept when a card still tests the same concept (so
existing progress carries over); new concepts got new ids.

---

## Thoracic (53 → 50 cards)

**Epidemiology & Risk Factors (8).** MK: NSCLC vs SCLC behavior; histology and
location (adeno peripheral, squamous/small cell central); why driver/PD-L1 testing
changes therapy; screening criteria. SK: radon in never-smokers; classic EGFR
patient; SCLC paraneoplastic syndromes. *Not cards:* death/incidence counts,
histology percentages, mutation frequencies, the full occupational exposure list,
the symptom-localization table (taught in staging/anatomy instead).

**Anatomy & Lymphatics (6).** MK: central vs ultracentral (sets SBRT dose);
lower-lobe motion; Pancoast anatomy; supraclavicular = N3 not M1. SK: station 7
always N2. *Not cards:* lobe/fissure/segment counts, RALS hilum, the R/L
paratracheal boundary, lobe-by-lobe drainage, skip-metastasis rates. The N1/N2/N3
framework lives in Workup & Staging so it is not tested twice.

**Imaging (7).** MK: what PET/CT adds; PET false positives need tissue; who gets
brain MRI; ITV/4D-CT; fibrosis vs recurrence after SBRT. SK: suspicious nodule
features; solid component sets T. *Not cards:* the four axial levels and ITMIG
compartments (interactive reading), SUV cutoffs, histology "fingerprints," Lung-RADS
categories, the 1 cm node threshold.

**Workup & Staging (10).** MK: biopsy for the highest stage; EBUS first; who skips
invasive staging; fitness decides surgery vs SBRT; T size cutoffs (3/5/7 cm); T3 vs
T4 as a principle; N1/N2/N3 framework; malignant effusion = M1a; limited vs
extensive SCLC. SK: the separate-nodule rule. *Not cards:* T1a-c/T2a-b, N2a vs N2b
(kept in Extra), M1c1 vs M1c2, every stage-group combination, ppo FEV1 bands, the
NCCN tab tables.

**Treatment Paradigms (19).** MK: operable vs inoperable N0; SBRT not for N+;
fractionation by location; ultracentral toxicity; unresectable stage III
(60 Gy/30 → durvalumab); RTOG 0617; involved-field targets; PORT only for a
positive margin; pneumonitis and esophagitis; oligometastatic SBRT; palliative
courses; LS-SCLC chemoRT; PCI; ES-SCLC. SK: BED10 ≥100 Gy; Pancoast trimodality;
neoadjuvant chemo-IO for resectable stage III. *Not cards:* individual regimen
lists per location beyond the 3/5/8 rule, HILUS dose, complete-response
percentages, every constraint in the table (only lung and esophagus kept),
IMpower133/CASPIAN details, palliative bone regimens.

**Workup & Staging (9).** MK: dental eval (ORN); p16 testing; T4a vs T4b; non-HPV

**Treatment (12).** MK: surgery first for oral cavity; adjuvant RT triggers; ENE /

not discuss toxicity (xerostomia, mucositis, dysphagia); the xerostomia card was kept
because it is must-know, but the reading may deserve a short toxicity section.

## Overview / Before You Start (78 → 64 cards)

The biggest issue here was redundancy between the "Before You Start" pages (How
Radiation Works, Radiation Modalities, Treatment Planning) and the deeper Overview
readings: the four R's had 7 cards, target volumes had 6, and SRS/SBRT, LDR/HDR,
IMRT/VMAT, and EBRT/brachy were each tested twice. Each concept now lives once, in
the section that teaches it at the right level: intro concepts in Before You Start,
mechanism-level detail in Biology/Physics, notation in Constraints/Acronyms.

*Not cards:* pair production, the four-interaction list, 53BP1/BRCA1 roles, the
DNA-repair syndrome list, the HYTEC fraction table, QUANTEC numbers beyond cord,
brainstem/optics, lung, and parotid, the GBM and NSCLC worked examples (taught in
the site decks), LINAC energy ranges, MLC delivery modes.
